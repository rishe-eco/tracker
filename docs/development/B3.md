## Built

- **`api/src/services/reflect/catches.ts`** (new): the matcher, copied from `noticing/catches.ts` with a docblock that says why (§0.3). `ARABIC_FOLDS` and `normalizeForMatch` are byte-identical to Noticing's, `LETTERISH` lookarounds with the `u` flag, and the longest trigger wins. `detectInterpretation(pack, story)` splits the original story on `.`, `!`, `?`, `؟` and line breaks, normalizes each sentence, and returns the lexicon's `word` and the person's own trimmed `sentence` (D-13, PD-13). It also holds the defensive `lastCatchAt` parse and the cooldown, and `interpretationOfferFor` and `fauxOfferFor`. Each writes its `*SurfacedAt` and its `lastCatchAt` key in one `$transaction`, and only when an offer is made. Packs come from the pinned versions in `ReflectState`, in `DIALS.practiceLocale` (D-16).
- **`api/src/services/reflect/entry.ts`:** `saveMoment` reads the stored moment's feeling before the upsert and asks for the faux offer only for a non-null feeling that differs from it. `move` asks for the interpretation offer only on a forward move into `reality`. Both run inside the existing `withUserLock` callback. No SDL or resolver change.
- **Tests:** `reflectCatches.unit.test.ts` (new, 14: AC-31, AC-28), `reflectCatches.integration.test.ts` (new, 17: AC-21, AC-25's server half, AC-29, AC-32, both cooldowns, the faux offer, on a fixed clock), the AC-26 imports fence in `reflectFences.unit.test.ts`, and the card's one changed test in `reflect.integration.test.ts` (`betrayed` now surfaces the faux offer). The review added a transport test and widened the fence (below).

No client file, `common.json` key, schema change, migration or dependency.

## Decided

The card's *Decided here*:

1. **Matching is per sentence; the longest trigger across sentences wins; the first sentence holding it is returned.** *Accepted.* Probed: `on\npurpose` doesn't match, and in `She always. He did it on purpose!` the longer `on purpose` wins from the second sentence.
2. **The sentence is the original, trimmed, with its terminator dropped.** *Accepted.* Probed: `on  purpose` (two spaces) comes back with both spaces, and `SHE NEVER CALLS` keeps its case. One consequence: `…` (U+2026) isn't a terminator, so `We ate… she always leaves` returns the whole line. That's acceptable, because the line is still the person's own.
3. **A fixture pack for the nesting test; the real draft `fa` pack for the Persian cases.** *Accepted.*
4. **Branch `reflect-b3-catches`, slot A.** *Accepted.*

The lane's own calls (report part 4):

1. **The helpers live in `catches.ts`, named `interpretationOfferFor` and `fauxOfferFor`.** *Accepted:* `entry.ts` stays thin, and neither name is one the fence refuses.
2. **A missing `ReflectState` means no offer.** *Accepted:* `startReflectEntry` always ensures it, so this can't happen through the API.
3. **A feeling saved as `""` is null and never matched.** *Accepted.*

6. **A bare-directory import (`../noticing`) is refused.** *Accepted:* `../feelingsNeeds` now is too.

The lane report's part 5, answered:

1. *The `betrayed` test change:* right; `betrayed` is a faux feeling in the `en` F&N pack.
2. *The fold doesn't map a curly apostrophe:* confirmed by probe. `He didn’t care at all.` (U+2019) returns null, and the straight-quote form matches. The fix is either a change to the shared fold (three copies, Noticing's included, and so a Noticing file) or a second authored trigger. Both are outside B3's *Not here*, so the choice goes to the founder (*Owed*).
3. *Back and then forward again after the cooldown lifts is held only by the entry gate:* that is the plan's "at most once per entry", and the test "one offer per entry across two forward moves and a Back" holds it.
