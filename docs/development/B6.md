## Built

- **The API half** (spec 0.2 moved it here; the plan's "client only" is out of date, as the card says):
  - `api/src/services/reflect/entry.ts`: `LISTABLE` is *closed, with any written text*: one Prisma `where` with an `OR` over scene, story, close, `some` moment with a when, feeling or need, and `some` reading with a reading or camera line. `hasPast`, `listPast` and `getPast` use the rule; `holdsText` is the same rule in JS for `getPast` and `hasWriting`. A row is `{ id, createdAt, firstWriting }`: the first writing in step order (scene, story, the first moment's when, the first reading, the close, then the first feeling, need and camera line), cut by the existing surrogate-safe `storyStart` at 80. `listPast` combines the rule and the cursor as `AND: [LISTABLE, cursor]`. `getPast` returns the entry without `step`.
  - `typeDefs.ts`: `ReflectPastEntry` (no `step`, no outcome, no after-entry answer), `ReflectPastRow.firstWriting` in place of `scene` and `storyStart`, `ReflectOpenEntry.hasWriting` in place of `hasStory`. `typeResolvers.ts` resolves `ReflectPastEntry` as it does `ReflectEntry`.
  - `reflectFences.unit.test.ts`: `ReflectPastEntry` in the guard list, and a fence refusing `step` or `outcome` on it, with planted violations.
- **The client half**, both pages unlinked from Tools (PD-5), reading through `useApi` only (D-9):
  - `ReflectPastListPage.tsx` (plate 10, `/tools/reflect/past`): row placeholders while loading; each row the date through `useAppDate` above the label, cut to one line, `dir="auto"`, the whole row one link; *Show older* under the last row when `hasOlder`, appending; the empty line and *Back to Reflect*; a couldn't-load line with Try again.
  - `ReflectPastEntryPage.tsx` (plate 11, `/tools/reflect/past/:id`): *Past entries* at the top, the date, then each written part under a short label (Scene, What happened, Moments, Readings, Looking at it now), parts never written left out; a moment's when on its own line, then feeling · need · Met; a reading and its camera line on two lines, the camera muted; *Delete* at the end through `ConfirmDialog`, then the list with `replace`; one not-found line for a stranger's id and a deleted one, read from `getLastError()`.
  - `ReflectHomePage.tsx`, `types.ts`, `queries.ts`: `hasWriting`; `GET_REFLECT_PAST_ENTRIES`, `GET_REFLECT_PAST_ENTRY`; `ReflectPastRow`, `ReflectPastPage`, `ReflectPastEntry`. Routes in `protectedRoutes.tsx`.
  - `reflect.past.*` in both `common.json` files, draft.
- **e2e:** `MODULE_KEYS` in one file, `client/e2e/helpers/moduleKeys.ts`, imported by `globalSetup.ts` and `helpers/freshUser.ts`. `reflect.spec.ts` adds three walks at 1280, 360 and 180 px, each on its own user.
- **Tests:** `reflect.integration.test.ts` (list, labels one fallback at a time, whitespace, the surrogate cut, the past entry without `step`, `hasWriting`), `reflectFences.unit.test.ts`, `client/app/test/ReflectPast.test.tsx` (9 from the lane, 1 from the review), `ReflectHome.test.tsx`.
- **The review's two commits:** `a082ee3` (the moment line's dots), `a94a14c` (*Unmet*). Under *Verification*.

No schema, migration, dependency or `.env` change.

## Decided

The card's *Decided here*:

1. **Whitespace-only text counts as written: listed, and labelled as it is.** *Accepted.* Probed: a story of three spaces is listed with a blank-looking label under its date, can be opened and deleted. The B4 open-work row is struck. The consequence the card names stands: an entry whose scene is only spaces and whose story is real is labelled by the spaces.
2. **The label's fallbacks after the spec's five: the first feeling, then need, then camera line.** *Accepted;* tested one at a time. The spec still names five (§4.10, AC-37): flagged for `revise`.
3. **`hasStory` renamed `hasWriting`.** *Accepted.* Over HTTP, `hasStory` is now a validation error, so an old client fails loudly rather than reading a wrong value; the client in this deploy asks for `hasWriting`.
4. **One shared e2e `MODULE_KEYS`.** *Accepted.* The B5 open-work row is struck.

The lane's own calls (report part 4):

1. **The label takes the first child with text, not `moments[0]`.** *Accepted:* it agrees with the spec whenever the first moment has text, and never leaves a listable entry unlabelled.
2. **One rule written three times** (`LISTABLE`, `holdsText`, the client's `holdsWrittenText`). *Accepted,* to keep `getPast` at one query. They agree because every write goes through `norm`, which stores `""` as null; checked by reading every write path in `entry.ts` (`saveEntry`, `saveMoment`, `saveReading`), and the integration tests drive both server copies with the same cases. A Met-only moment counts in none of them (probed: `Not found`).
3. **`AND: [LISTABLE, cursor]`.** *Accepted:* both carry an `OR`, and a spread would have let the cursor's replace the rule's. The cursor test pages 50 with equal timestamps.
4. **`getPast` strips `step` from the object too.** *Accepted.* Other columns (`afterAnswer`, the `*SurfacedAt` stamps) stay on the object, and the type doesn't expose them: over HTTP `afterAnswer` and `step` on `ReflectPastEntry` are validation errors.
5. **Not found from `getLastError()`.** *Accepted:* the hook's `error` state is a render behind after `await call()`.
6. **Met through new `reflect.past.met.*` keys.** *Accepted, with one word changed* (`a94a14c`): `en` read *Not met*, but the feelings step offers the served *Met · Unmet · Not sure* and plate 11 draws *Unmet*, so the person read back a word they didn't choose. `fa` is unchanged.
7. **A reading and its camera line on two lines, no arrow.** *Accepted:* plate 11's `←` would point the wrong way in one of the two directions.
8. **No header back link on the list.** *Accepted:* plate 10 draws none; *Back to Reflect* shows in the empty state, and the app's nav is always there.
9. **The e2e walks sign up their own users and delete nothing of the shared one's.** *Accepted.*
10. **Draft `fa`.** *Accepted as draft.* For the Persian reader: the page's button is *پاکش کن* while the confirm's is the shared *حذف* (`common.delete`); *صحنه* is the label for Scene here and the step bar's name for the story step (B4's key).
