**Findings, both fixed on the branch:**

1. **The faux offer outlived its word** (`0d6606b`). With the own-word field focused, a tap on a palette chip blurs the field first, so the own word is committed and its save can return the offer; then the chip's word is saved, and its result, carrying no offer, left the offer about the old word under the new feeling. *Use one* would then replace a palette word the person had just chosen. A feeling save with no offer now drops the offer at its position, unless it saved the same word that surfaced it. Two tests: the chip after the blur (failed first), and the same word committed again keeps the offer (a guard).
