## Built

- **`api/prisma/schema.prisma`:** the block `Reflect · the observer recount`, between F&N's `LoopState` and Noticing's banner (D-2): `ReflectEntry`, `ReflectMoment`, `ReflectReading`, `ReflectState`, each with a doc comment, and `reflectEntries` and `reflectState` on `User`. `ReflectState.lastCatchAt` carries convention 13's comment.
- **Migration `20261021133354_add_reflect`:** four `CREATE TABLE`s with cascading foreign keys, the `(userId, closedAt)` index and three unique indexes. No other table is touched: no `ALTER`, `DROP` or `PRAGMA`.
- **`api/src/content/reflect/`:** `types.ts`, `dials.ts`, `index.ts` (`getReflectPack(version, locale)`, cached, refusing an unknown version or locale), `v1/spec.ts` (the four family ids), `v1/index.ts`, `v1/surface.en.ts` (`reviewed`) and `v1/surface.fa.ts` (`draft`). The surfaces hold the scene and story prompts, the moment labels, the reality prompt and pair labels, the close prompt, the offer line with its `{word}` slot and two buttons, and the lexicon: `en` 10, 12, 10 and 10 triggers, `fa` 13, 13, 14 and 13.
- **`api/src/__tests__/reflectContent.unit.test.ts`:** AC-42 (key parity), AC-19 (the forbidden words, `fa` on normalized text), the lexicon's shape and cross-family nesting, the registry and the dials. At the review it gained two tests: the pack holds no §6.5 interface string, and `en` carries the spec's labels.
- **`api/src/__tests__/reflectFences.unit.test.ts`:** the schema half (placement before Noticing's banner, no index or unique key on a text column, no counting field, no relation to `Journal`, `Goal`, `Project` or `JournalAccess`) and the imports half (AC-45, and AC-28's browser half). At the review the schema scanner learned more forms, and AC-45's check came to follow imports (below).

No API, resolver, screen, string, client file or dependency changed.
