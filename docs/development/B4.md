## Built

- **Routes** `/tools/reflect` and `/tools/reflect/entry` in `client/app/protectedRoutes.tsx`. They are live but unlinked (PD-5).
- **Documents** in `client/app/api/queries.ts`: `GET_REFLECT_HOME`, `GET_REFLECT_ENTRY`, `GET_REFLECT_CONTENT`, and `START_`, `SAVE_`, `MOVE_`, `STOP_`, `DELETE_REFLECT_ENTRY`. They select only §6.1's fields, and no pre-check answer, outcome or catch.
- **`client/app/components/reflect/`:**
  - `types.ts`: the shapes the client reads, and `holdsWrittenText` (AC-56).
  - `useReflectAutosave.ts`: each field is saved 800 ms after typing stops, through one ordered queue. `flush()` rejects on a failed save, and `retry()` sends the text again. It also flushes on unmount and never touches browser storage.
  - `ReflectShell.tsx`: the banner, the step bar with Stop here at the inline end, the footer, and the not-saved line.
  - `ReflectTextInput.tsx`: the prompt as the label (isolated at the review), `dir="auto"`, `maxLength` at the served bound, the bound sentence and the soft-line sentence.
  - `PrecheckStep.tsx`: chips that clear on a second tap, and the advisory that swaps Next for Not now and Keep going (wrapping at narrow widths since the review). No Back.
  - `StoryStep.tsx`: the scene and the story under the pack's prompts. No Back.
  - `CalmClose.tsx`: the draft copy, Back to Reflect, and Delete what I wrote behind `ConfirmDialog` (destructive). Delete and the "kept" line are hidden when nothing was written.
  - `ReflectEntryPage.tsx`: the load, the step switch, and `commitStep` (flush first, then the pre-check save on every exit). Then Next, Stop here and delete, the banner rule, and the redirect when no entry is open.
  - `ReflectHomePage.tsx`: the placeholder, Start, or Continue with Start a new one (`replacing`) and G4's line. Also the Past entries link and the banner.
- **Copy:** a `reflect` key in both `common.json` files. The calm close is under its final `reflect.calmClose.*` keys, with `_draft` beside them.
- **Tests:** `client/app/test/ReflectHome.test.tsx` (6) and `ReflectEntry.test.tsx` (17 from the lane, plus 1 from the review), and `client/e2e/reflect.spec.ts` (the plan's three walks).
- **The review's two fixes:** `cf9342b` isolates the prompt's direction in its label, and `0a88e67` wraps the advisory's two buttons at narrow widths (both under *Verification*).

No API, schema, migration or dependency change.

**Findings, both fixed on the branch:**

1. **The English prompts in a Persian page had their punctuation at the wrong end** (`cf9342b`). The labels read "?Where were you, and who was there" and ".after another" because the label took the page's `rtl`. The prompt now sits in a `dir="auto"` span inside the label. The line still aligns right, and only the punctuation moves. The review's test failed first and passes now. Screenshot `fa-360-05-story-empty`, before and after.
2. **At 180 px, the advisory's Keep going was pushed out of the column** (`0a88e67`). It ran to 226 px in a 180 px column in English, and to -6 px in Persian, where it was clipped and out of reach. The pair now wraps, aligned to the inline end. Nothing changes at 360 px, where the pair fits on one line (the positions were the same before and after).

**Probes** (a temporary hook test, deleted after; 6 of 6 held):

- A failed save, more typing, then Try again sends only the latest text, and the not-saved line clears.
- A later autosave of the failed field clears the line without Try again.
- Typing during a flush that is in flight is saved before the flush resolves, in order.
- When the scene fails and the story saves, the flush rejects, and Try again resends the scene alone.
- An unmount with text waiting still sends it.
- A save that throws counts as failed and keeps the text.
- The fields' `maxLength` matches the served bounds (140 and 5000), and the server counts `.length` too, so the client can never send more than the server takes.
